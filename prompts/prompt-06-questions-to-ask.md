# P6 — 5 Smart Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Use it for: End the interview strong with grouped, thoughtful questions

From AI Made Simple — all prompts tested, outputs shown unedited.

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```text
I'm interviewing for a Customer Support Representative job tomorrow. Give me 5 smart questions to ask the interviewer at the end of the interview. Group them by topic: About the role / About the team / About success. Add a one-line note under each question explaining why it works. Then finish by naming the ONE question I should ask if there's only time for one, and why.
```

## Real AI output (unedited)

About the role
1. "What would a typical week look like for someone in this position?"
Why it works: It shows you're picturing yourself doing the job, and the answer tells you what the day really feels like.
2. "What are the busiest times for the support team, and how is the workload shared?"
Why it works: You learn the real pressure points before you accept, and you sound like someone who plans ahead.

About the team
3. "How big is the support team, and who would I go to when I'm stuck on a hard case?"
Why it works: It signals you want to learn fast and use the team, not struggle alone.
4. "How does the team give feedback — is there regular coaching or review of chats and emails?"
Why it works: Asking about feedback marks you as coachable, which support managers prize.

About success
5. "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?"
Why it works: It turns the end of the interview into a conversation about you hitting their targets.

If there's only time for ONE question, ask #5: "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?" It makes the interviewer imagine you in the role, succeeding — and their answer tells you exactly what to deliver if you get the job.
