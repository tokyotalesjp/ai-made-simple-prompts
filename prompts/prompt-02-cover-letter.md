# P2 — Cover Letter Opening Paragraph

Use it for: Write a confident cover letter opening from a job posting

From AI Made Simple — all prompts tested, outputs shown unedited.

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```text
job posting (Customer Support Representative) + "Write the opening paragraph (about 60 words) of a cover letter for a candidate named Alex who has 2 years of retail customer service experience."
```

## Real AI output (unedited)

"Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the Customer Support Representative position. With two years of retail customer service experience, I have developed strong communication skills, patience, and a customer-first approach to resolving problems. I am confident in my ability to provide clear, friendly support through email and chat while addressing customer questions and billing concerns professionally."
