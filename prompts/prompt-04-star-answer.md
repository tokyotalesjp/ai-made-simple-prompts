# P4 — STAR Interview Answer Rewrite

Use it for: Turn a vague self-pitch into a structured STAR answer

From AI Made Simple — all prompts tested, outputs shown unedited.

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```text
Rewrite this interview answer using the STAR method. The candidate works in retail customer service. Keep the answer under 90 words, in first person, and label the four parts (Situation / Task / Action / Result). Do NOT invent numbers or facts — use [brackets] where a detail needs to be filled in.

My answer now: "I'm a hard worker, I'm good with customers, and my manager said I was reliable."
```

## Real AI output (unedited)

Situation: My retail store was often short-staffed during [busy sale periods], and checkout lines built up fast.
Task: I had to keep the line moving while still helping customers on the floor.
Action: I rotated between the register and the floor, restocked fast-selling items between rushes, and answered common questions on the spot.
Result: Lines cleared faster, customers got help instead of waiting — and my manager started giving me the busiest shifts, because the front end was covered.
