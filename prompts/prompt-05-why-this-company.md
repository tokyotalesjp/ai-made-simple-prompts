# P5 — Why Do You Want to Work Here?

Use it for: Draft a specific, human answer that connects posting to experience

From AI Made Simple — all prompts tested, outputs shown unedited.

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```text
Write a draft answer to "Why do you want to work here?" (about 80 words, first person) for the candidate below. Connect ONE specific detail from the job posting to ONE real thing from the candidate's experience. Sound like a person, not a template. Do NOT invent company facts, awards, or numbers — use [brackets] for anything that must be checked.

Job posting (Customer Support Representative): "Answer customer questions by email and chat. Help customers with billing concerns. We value friendly, clear communication."
Candidate: 2 years of retail customer service experience — helping customers in person, answering product questions, and resolving complaints.
My current answer: "I want to work at your company because it is a famous company, and I think I can grow here."
```

## Real AI output (unedited)

Honestly, this posting describes the part of my current job I like most. For two years in retail, the work I've enjoyed — and been praised for — is answering customers' questions and talking an unhappy customer through a problem until it's resolved. Your posting asks for exactly that, by email and chat instead of face-to-face. I want to take the communication skills I've built in person and use them where I can help even more people each day — starting with [one true detail about this company, in your own words].
