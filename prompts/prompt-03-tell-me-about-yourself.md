# P3 — Tell Me About Yourself — 4-Sentence Formula

Use it for: Answer the most common interview opener with a clear structure

From AI Made Simple — all prompts tested, outputs shown unedited.

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```text
Give me a simple 4-sentence structure for answering "Tell me about yourself" in a job interview, then one example answer (about 70 words) for a retail worker applying for a customer support job.
```

## Real AI output (unedited)

\nStructure:
1. Who you are: Briefly introduce your professional background and experience.
2. What you've done: Highlight relevant skills and responsibilities from your previous work.
3. Your strengths: Explain what you do well and how you help customers or solve problems.
4. Why this job: Connect your experience to the position you're applying for.

Example: "I have two years of experience working in retail customer service, where I've helped customers, answered questions, and resolved complaints. Through this experience, I've developed strong communication, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills. I enjoy helping people and staying calm when handling challenging situations. I'm now looking to move into customer support, where I can use my experience to provide excellent service and build positive customer relationships through email and chat."
